  • The avalanche bulletin (BERA) is Météo-France's, shown verbatim. It describes a massif, not a slope. Always check the official source on meteofrance.com.
  • "Today's verdict" is written with the help of an AI from weather models and reviewed by the Alpow team.
  • Snow reports come from other skiers and describe what they saw, not the state of every slope. Only post what you saw on site.
