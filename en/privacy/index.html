Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Alpow has no account, no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

  • Favourite resorts stay on your iPhone. The app asks the server for those resorts' forecasts without attaching any identifier.
  • Anonymous device ID: a random ID created by the app, not linked to you or your Apple ID. It is used to deliver the alerts you asked for and to rate-limit reports. Reports show an author code derived from this ID and the resort, so two reports at two different resorts cannot be linked. An ID that has not contacted the server for 13 months is deleted from it, with its reports, alerts and push token.
  • Alerts: when you turn on an alert, the app sends the server your Apple push token (APNs), the app language, the resorts and thresholds you chose and, for BERA alerts, the massifs you follow, the minimum danger level, the "only when rising" choice and your quiet hours. The server keeps a record of the notifications it sent so it never sends the same one twice. All of it is deleted from the server when you turn every alert off, and as soon as Apple rejects the token (app deleted, expired token).
  • Location: requested "while using the app", the first time you use a feature that needs it: "Near me" (first launch and search), your position on the map, the Emergency page, a snow report or a ski day. It is read and used on the iPhone: sorting nearby resorts, showing you on the map, displaying your coordinates for rescuers, checking that you are inside the ski area before a report. No coordinates or GPS track are sent: for a report, the server receives the resort and the time you were there, which amounts to a coarse location ("at this resort, at this time").
  • Ski day (if you start one): GPS, including with the screen locked while the day runs, and the barometer count your runs, vertical, distance, top speed and lifts ridden. Everything is computed and kept on your iPhone. Nothing is sent, and the GPS track is not kept, only the summary, which you can delete in Settings › My ski days. A shared image contains only that summary.
  • Snow reports: the resort, the tags you picked (e.g. "Powder", "Wind"), the time you were on site and the time you sent it. No photos, no free text. They are shown for 48 hours and deleted from the server after 30 days.
  • App Attest: Apple attests that the request comes from a genuine copy of the app. The server keeps the public key your iPhone generated for this; no personal data is sent.
  • Verdict of the day: drafted with the help of an AI (Anthropic) from the weather forecasts, then reviewed. No user data is sent to that service.
  • Base maps: IGN maps (plan and slopes) load directly from IGN's Géoplateforme (data.geopf.fr), which sees your IP address and the area on screen, like any web server. The Apple map comes from Apple Maps.
  • "My friends" link: it only contains the resort, the day and, if you type one, a first name. You send it to whoever you like; the page opens on GitHub Pages or in the app, and Alpow receives nothing.
  • Departure city and drive times (if you pick a "≤ 1 h", "≤ 2 h" or "≤ 3 h" filter): the city you choose, its centre and the drive times stay on your iPhone; they are never sent to Alpow, nor put in the ranking's address (which only carries the period). The city search and the drive times are computed by Apple Maps, which therefore receives the departure city and each resort's arrival point, under Apple's policy. Drive times are kept for an hour on the iPhone; "Forget my city" erases them with the city.
  • Piste map: the PDF or image is downloaded straight from the resort's official site, which sees your IP address like any web server; Alpow does not rehost it and receives nothing. The file is kept in the iPhone's cache so it stays readable offline; iOS may clear it when space runs low.
  • Directions: the button opens Apple Maps with the resort's car park as destination; Maps computes the route from your location under Apple's policy. Alpow does not receive your location for this.
  • Emergency page (112 button at the top of the screen): it shows your GPS position (coordinates, altitude, accuracy) so you can read it to rescuers. The position is read and shown on the iPhone only: nothing is sent or stored. "Copy" and "Send by SMS" are actions you take through the system; Alpow receives nothing.
  • External and affiliate links: links to official resort sites and to any partner shop open in Safari; Alpow adds no identifier about you. An affiliate link, always marked "Affiliate link", only carries Alpow's partner code so the shop knows the visit came from Alpow. What the shop does next falls under its own policy. No affiliate link is active today.
  • Hosting: the Alpow server is hosted in France by the publisher. Requests pass through Cloudflare, which routes the traffic. The Alpow server does not record IP addresses: it only keeps in memory, for an hour at most, a counter per address to limit how many anonymous IDs can be created.

You can erase everything at any time in the app: Me › Settings › Erase my data. This deletes the ID, reports, the entries you reported, alerts and push token from the server, then creates a new ID. If erasing fails (no network, for instance), the app tells you and nothing is erased on the iPhone: try again. The server's encrypted backups are kept 30 days, so erased data leaves them within 30 days. One exception: if the device was blocked for abuse, the server keeps, for 13 months at most, the random ID, the App Attest key ID and the block, with no report, alert or token, so erasing does not lift the block.

Controller: bonout. To exercise your rights (access, correction, erasure, objection) or ask a question, see the Support page. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Alpow does not replace the official bulletin or your own judgement. In the mountains, you are responsible for your decisions.
