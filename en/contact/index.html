Contact and support

A question, a bug, a missing resort or a problematic snow report: write to support@alpow.app. We answer within a few days.

To report an abusive snow report, you can also use "Report this entry" in the app: a report flagged three times disappears.

To erase your data: Settings › Erase my data, in the app.

Publisher: bonout, France.

Alpow does not replace the official bulletin or your own judgement. In the mountains, you are responsible for your decisions.
